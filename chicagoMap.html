About This Map

The data comes from a Google My Maps project exported as KML. The map organizes places into three layers: a general Chicago to-do list, food spots, and activities. Each marker shows the place name and any notes (type, neighborhood, address) captured when it was saved.

The map tiles are served by OpenStreetMap and rendered with Leaflet. Address locations were geocoded with Nominatim.
