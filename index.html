I earned my Sc.M. in Computer Science from Brown University in May 2025, after my Sc.B. in CS there in 2024, focusing on generative AI, AI ethics, and software development.

On this site you'll find two LLM apps I built and deployed, an agent with tool calling and a structured-output trip planner, plus ML research, data analysis, and UX work. Across all of it, I care about making technology that is accessible to everyone.
